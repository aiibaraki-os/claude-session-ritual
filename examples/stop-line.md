## Rules for the call
- Use confirmed numbers only: no projections, no effort, no gut feeling.
- A day I could not measure counts as "unmeasured". It argues for neither stopping nor continuing.
- Stop means freeze. Keep the work. Reconsider once, 180 days later.
- Change this sheet only with the reason written below it. No quiet loosening.
